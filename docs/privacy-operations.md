# Privacy operations for the hosted portfolio demo

Giacomo Negri handles requests at the private email contact published in the bilingual privacy notice. The hosted demo accepts fictional webhook content. Visitors’ real account details, network requests and correspondence still require protection. The controller is responsible for the actions below. Keep case files, contracts and evidence in restricted storage outside this repository.

## When someone contacts the operator

1. Record the date received, requested right, due date and a minimal reference. Accept requests without a login, including from a person whose information arrived in a webhook. Use a proportionate identity check if there is reasonable doubt. Never ask for a password, MFA code, signing secret or complete webhook. Use the event ID, source and approximate time to locate a reported submission.
2. Answer within one calendar month. For a complex request or many simultaneous requests, explain any extension and its reasons within the first month. The extension can be up to two further months. Document any refusal, its reasons, and the available complaint and judicial remedies. Route access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objections individually.
3. The profile download covers personal account information in the app. For an access request, also check the relevant mailbox, restricted logs, retained backups and any workspace metadata concerning the requester. Review information about third parties before disclosure. Provide an appropriately protected response, including applicable recipients, purposes, retention and data sources.
4. If real customer information reached a test workspace, stop further processing of the affected event where possible. Coordinate with the workspace owner and actual recipients, check pending and in-flight deliveries, and document any copies that still require action. Apply rectifications, restrictions or erasure as appropriate. Notify recipients under Art. 19 except where impossible or disproportionate. Keep evidence of the decision and action with a defined review/expiry date.
5. Record the minimum needed to reconcile any later restore. Review copies in Gmail (including Sent and Trash), provider logs, and recovery archives separately from live PostgreSQL. Classify any material that must be retained and explain the basis and expected expiry. An account deletion in the app cannot recall an email or an already delivered webhook.

## Personal data breaches

Older forms depended on JavaScript to select POST. A browser using the native fallback could put form fields in a GET URL. For incident review, check available access-log metadata privately for the presence of sensitive parameter names on affected routes. Avoid extracting parameter values into reports. Record the evidence and available log window before deciding whether an exposure occurred.

Contain the incident, preserve minimal evidence privately and record the awareness time, affected people and data, consequences and containment. Assess the risk to people and document the decision for every breach. Unless the risk is unlikely, notify the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness. Explain delays and supply missing facts in stages. For high risk, inform affected people without undue delay unless an Article 34 exception applies. Test the contact route and recovery procedure with synthetic fixtures.
